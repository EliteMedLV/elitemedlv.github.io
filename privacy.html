Travel Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026. Applies to the Travel Companion website and iOS app.

Travel Companion is a family trip-planning app operated by an individual, not a company. It has no advertising, no analytics trackers, and it never sells or shares your data with anyone. Everything it stores exists only so the app can do what you asked it to do.

What the app stores

DataWhyWhere it comes from
Your name, email address and account identifier To sign you in and keep your trips separate from everyone else's Google or Apple, when you sign in

Google Calendar access

Connecting your calendar is optional. If you do, the app requests the calendar scope so it can read upcoming events and, when you ask it to, create or update events for trip items. The refresh token Google issues is stored encrypted on the server and is never sent to your browser or phone. You can revoke access at any time from the app (Disconnect) or from your Google Account's third-party access page.

Travel Companion's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it lives

Data is stored in a Supabase project (PostgreSQL) hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States, protected by row-level security so that an account can only ever read its own rows. The website is served by GitHub Pages. Traffic is encrypted in transit.

Who can see it

You, and the operator of the app when administering the database. No third parties receive your data, with the exception of the service providers above, who process it on the app's behalf under their own privacy terms.

Deleting your data

Open the app, and use Delete account in the top bar. This revokes the Google Calendar grant, erases every row that belongs to you, and deletes the account itself, immediately and irreversibly. Backups held by the hosting provider expire within seven days.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning family travel. Family-member profiles may describe children, but children do not have accounts of their own.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above moves and the app shows a notice on next sign-in. Questions go to the support contact listed on the App Store page, or to the operator directly if they shared the app with you.
